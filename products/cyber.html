FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is cyber insurance mandatory in Indonesia?+

No. UU PDP requires you to protect personal data but doesn't mandate insurance. Cyber cover manages the financial risk if a breach happens anyway.

What do insurers require to offer cover?+

Commonly multi-factor authentication (MFA), endpoint detection (EDR), tested backups and staff training. We help you meet these to unlock better terms.

How much does cyber insurance cost?+

It's priced on your chosen coverage limit, revenue, sector and security posture rather than asset value. Get a quote and we'll find the best fit.
