The basics

What is employee benefit?

Employee benefit refers to the non-cash compensation, facilities and protection a company provides to its staff on top of their base salary, in line with their employment agreement. It goes beyond monthly pay, annual bonuses or holiday allowances — it's the package of perks, security and wellbeing support that makes people want to join and stay.

In Indonesia, employers are legally required to enrol staff in BPJS Kesehatan and BPJS Ketenagakerjaan. Many companies build on that mandatory base with private group health, life and accident cover to offer stronger, faster and more comprehensive protection — and to stand out as an employer.

Why employee benefits matter for your business

Even smaller companies gain a real edge from a well-designed benefits programme. The impact shows up in three ways:

  • Productivity & wellbeing — staff who feel protected against illness or accident worry less and focus more, working with greater confidence and efficiency.
  • Retention — benefits signal that you value your people, building loyalty so your best employees are less tempted by competitors.
  • Attraction — a strong package makes your company more appealing and professional to high-quality candidates during recruitment.
