FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Single shipment or annual cover?+

If you ship regularly, an annual open cover is cheaper and automatic. One-off shipments can be insured per trip.

Does it cover international routes?+

Yes — import and export shipments worldwide, using standard Institute Cargo Clauses.

What are Institute Cargo Clauses?+

They define the level of cover: Clause A is the widest (all-risk), B and C cover named perils.
