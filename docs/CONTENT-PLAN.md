# Insurewise — SEO Content Plan

A keyword-mapped backlog for the Insights section. Each row = one bilingual (EN/ID)
article in the same template as the current posts. Hand me any topic and I'll write,
optimise and push it.

## How to use this
- **Cadence:** aim for 1 article every 1–2 weeks. Consistency beats bursts.
- **Priority:** "Quick win" = low competition, high intent — publish these first.
- **Each article should:** target one primary keyword in the H1/title, answer one
  clear question, include an FAQ block, and link to the matching product + quote page.
- **Already published (5):** employee benefit (what & why); HR survival guide 2026
  (KAPJ/BPJS/KRIS); property (+Iran war); liability; cargo (+Hormuz).

## Two highest-leverage non-article moves (recap)
1. **Backlinks** — get listed by insurer partners ("find a broker"), AAUI/APPARINDO,
   local business directories, and chambers of commerce. Biggest long-term ranking lever.
2. **Google Business Profile + reviews** — fastest path to local-pack visibility for
   "broker asuransi Jakarta" type searches.
