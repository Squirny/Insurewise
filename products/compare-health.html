How these prices work
  • Each price is the insurer's per-person annual rate for your average age band (19–30, 31–40, 41–55, 56–60) times your number of employees.
  • The insurer prices the final policy member by member, so a team with a wide age spread may come out higher or lower.
  • Hospital network counts come from the insurer's provider list in our clinic finder.
  • General information, not a binding offer. Your Insurewise advisor confirms exact benefits, exclusions and premium before you buy.

Questions

How are the prices calculated?+

Each priced plan uses the insurer's own per-person annual rate for the age band that matches your workforce's average age, multiplied by the number of employees. The insurer calculates the final premium on your actual member list, so it can differ.

What does the room limit per night mean?+

It is the room and board amount the plan pays per night in hospital. Surgeon, specialist and ICU fees are usually scaled to the room class, so an employee who takes a room above their limit can face a much bigger bill than just the room difference. Read more.

Why do some insurers show 'price on request'?+

We only show instant prices where we hold the insurer's real rate card. For other partners, our advisor requests a quote for you and compares it against the plans listed here.

Does this replace BPJS Kesehatan?+

No. BPJS Kesehatan is mandatory. Private group cover sits on top of it with higher room limits and faster access to private hospitals. BPJS vs private explained.
