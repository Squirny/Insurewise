Questions

Before you message us

Does my travel insurance cover me while I live in Bali?

Often not. Most travel policies are written for trips, with a maximum length per journey, and may not pay once you're a resident. Check your policy wording, or send it to us and we'll check it for you.

Is the comparison really free?

Yes. You pay nothing for the comparison and you're under no obligation to buy.

Which insurers do you work with?

We work with 20+ insurer partners, covering local plans paid in rupiah and international plans paid in USD or EUR. We'll show you which ones fit your situation.

Can I talk to someone in Italian? Parlate italiano?

Yes. We have a dedicated hotline in English and Italian, so you can go through your policy, your comparison and any claim in the language you're most comfortable with. Sì, parliamo italiano: scriveteci su WhatsApp.

Can you cover pregnancy and childbirth?

Many family plans exclude maternity or need a waiting period first. Tell us when you get in touch and we'll only show plans that match.

I rent my villa out. Does that change my insurance?

Usually, yes. Rental use and guest injuries are often excluded from a standard home policy. We'll check whether your cover includes commercial use and liability.

Do you also insure businesses in Bali?

Yes. Our core work is business insurance: staff health cover, property and liability for cafés, studios, clubs and other small businesses. Choose "My staff / business" in the form.
