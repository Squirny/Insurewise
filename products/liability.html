FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between public and product liability?+

Public liability covers injury/damage at your premises or operations; product liability covers harm caused by products you sell.

Do clients require it?+

Frequently — corporate clients, malls and government tenders often require minimum liability limits.

Is professional indemnity included?+

It's a separate but related cover for advice-based businesses; we can bundle it for you.
