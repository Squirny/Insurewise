FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions from Indonesian SME business owners.

What is the difference between Insurewise and going directly to an insurer? ▼
As an independent broker, we compare products from 10+ insurers on your behalf and recommend the best fit for your needs and budget. You get expert advice, better negotiated rates, and a claims advocate — all at no extra cost to you (we are paid by the insurer as commission, disclosed transparently).
How quickly can I get a quote? ▼
Our online quote form generates an indicative estimate instantly. A qualified advisor will then contact you within 24 hours to confirm the details and present formal quotes from our insurer panel.
Is employee benefit insurance mandatory in Indonesia? ▼
BPJS Kesehatan and BPJS Ketenagakerjaan are mandatory for all employers in Indonesia. However, many companies supplement this with commercial group health insurance to offer better hospital networks, higher benefit limits, and outpatient coverage — which significantly improves employee retention and satisfaction.
How does the client portal work? ▼
When you become a client, we create a secure portal account for you. All quotes, policy documents, payment records, and claim files are stored there. You can also initiate claims, upload documents, and communicate directly with your account manager — accessible from desktop or mobile 24/7.
Do you serve businesses outside Jakarta? ▼
Yes — our services are available nationwide across Indonesia. While our team is based in Jakarta, we work with clients in Surabaya, Bali, Medan, Bandung, and all other major cities. Our fully digital process means location is not a barrier.
Can I pay premiums in installments? ▼
Yes. Many of our insurer partners offer quarterly or semi-annual payment options for annual policies. We'll present the available payment terms alongside each quote so you can choose what works best for your cash flow.
